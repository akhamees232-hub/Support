Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Please review the following points to understand how data is handled within the app:

  • Data Collection: This app does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal or sensitive data to any external servers or third parties.
  • Data Storage: All data, information, and profiles you enter to build your resume are saved and processed locally and exclusively on your personal device.
  • Exported Files (PDF): When you export your resume as a PDF, the file is generated and saved entirely on your device. We have no access to your files.
  • Permissions: The app may request permission to access your device storage solely to allow you to save the exported PDF files or to upload a personal photo for your resume.
  • Changes to Policy: We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
